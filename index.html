Pick a format (Test, ODI or T20), your nation and opponent, call the coin toss, then choose to bat or bowl. Match length depends on the format (Test is two innings each, in whites with a red ball). Batting: tap as the ball crosses the dashed line. Better timing means more runs. You are only out if the ball hits the stumps; balls that miss them are wides. Bowling: drag the circle to where you want the ball to land, then tap Fast, Swing or Spin. Landing on the stumps line gives the best chance of a wicket, but bowl too wide and it is a wide.

Free play: bat against endless balls and chase a high score. Get out and your count restarts; your best is kept for the session. Difficulty changes the opponent’s speed, accuracy and wicket chances.

Camera: switch on First person (or press V) to see through the batter’s helmet when batting, or the bowler’s eyes when bowling. When bowling in this view, drag the target circle on the pitch.

Captains change often, so check the names in the TEAMS list at the top of the code.
